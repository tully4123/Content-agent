# Content-agent
Scores posts — feed it a Meta Business Suite CSV and it applies PubCam's real weighted engagement model (shares ×5, follows ×6, saves ×4, comments ×3, likes ×1, divided by reach, percentile-ranked against all post history) and flags what's working vs. what to retire
Ideas backlog — /capture logs content ideas from anywhere; performance insights and trend research feed the same list
Weekly schedule — builds a proposed posting schedule from approved ideas; a human approves every step and does the actual posting
Reports — checks results against the brand's strategy assumptions and proposes updates when data disagrees
Drive sync — pushes the schedule to Google Drive
